### Where to report problems

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Geekbench 7 for Linux (unofficial) is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
> supported by** Primate Labs.
>
> - **Do not send wrapper or packaging issues to Primate Labs.** Report them at
>   https://github.com/ventura8/geekbench7-linux/issues.
> - If a problem is in Geekbench itself, contact Primate Labs through their site.

This project downloads the official tarball from `cdn.geekbench.com` on your
machine and **never redistributes or patches** Geekbench.
